Notch Mode
==========

Notch Mode anchors your bar inside the display notch area at the top of the screen. On MacBooks with a notch, the bar splits around the physical notch. On external displays, ExtraBar creates a centered "virtual notch" area instead.

Setting up Notch Mode
---------------------

1. Open ExtraBar → **Settings** (⚙) → **Position**
2. Select the **Notch** card

You can also toggle it from the ExtraBar app menu with **Enable Notch Mode** (``⌘N``).

Options
-------

When Notch is selected, these options appear below the position picker:

- **Background** — Choose **Solid** or **Floating** for the notch bar background.
- **Size** — Small, Medium, or Large.
- **Auto-hide** — The bar hides automatically and reappears when you hover the notch area. Auto-hide is exclusive to Notch Mode.
- **Show on All Screens** — Display the notch bar on every connected monitor.
- **Display picker** — When "Show on All Screens" is off, choose which screen the bar appears on.

Keyboard navigation
-------------------

Keyboard navigation works in Notch Mode just like in the other positions — see :doc:`keyboard`.
